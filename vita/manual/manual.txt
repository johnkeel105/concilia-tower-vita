## Controls
Outside menus and dialogs:
  Left stick: scroll the camera
  Right stick: move the mouse pointer
  Cross or R: left click (hold to drag, e.g. floors or offices)
  L: right click (hold and move to drag the camera)
  Circle: Hand (pointer/select) tool
  Triangle: Inspect (magnifying glass) tool
  Square: Bulldozer tool
  D-pad: fine pointer nudge; with a build item selected,
    one tile left/right or one floor up/down (hold to repeat)
  Front touch: tap to click, drag to drag
  Start: open or close the top menu
  Select: debug labels
In menus, dialogs and this manual:
  D-pad: move the yellow focus box
  Cross or Circle: confirm / press the focused button (OK)
  Triangle: cancel and close without confirming
Touch and the pointer also work everywhere.

## Building
1. Pick an item from the Tools window or the Build Res. / Build Com. /
   Transport / Services menus. The pointer shows a crosshair.
2. Aim with the right stick or touch; the D-pad steps exactly one tile
   or one floor at a time.
3. Press Cross (or R / tap) to place. Hold and drag to lay a row of
   floors, offices or lobby in one go.
Everything above ground needs floor under it, and each new floor must sit
on the one below. The cost shows at the bottom of the Tools window; you
cannot build without enough funds.
Bulldozer (Square) removes what you click. Circle returns to the Hand.

## Tools and Tower Info
Tools window: Play/Pause at the top, then Bulldozer, Hand and Inspect,
then the build items. Items with a small corner triangle open a flyout
with related items (hotel rooms, food, transport and so on). New items
unlock as your tower earns stars.
Hand: the normal pointer. Double-click an elevator shaft for its settings.
Inspect: click a tenant or person to see details, rent and evaluation.
Tower Info (top right): clock, star rating, date, funds and population,
plus messages about events in your tower.
Map (left): click to jump the camera. The tabs switch between the plain
view, evaluation (blue good / yellow so-so / red bad), rent and hotel.
Drag any window by its title bar.

## Tool Sizing
Top menu > Tool Sizing changes only the Tools window and its buttons:
  Original size (1x), 25% larger (default) or 50% larger.
The check mark shows the current choice. It is saved to
ux0:data/concilia-tower/prefs.cfg and kept between sessions.
If 50% does not fit with many items unlocked, the window is shrunk just
enough to stay on screen. The Map is never resized.

## Saving and loading
Game > Save writes your current slot. The default save is
ux0:data/concilia-tower/concilliatower.sav and loads automatically at start.
Game > Save As... lets you pick a slot (tower-01.sav ... tower-16.sav)
or overwrite an existing save. Game > Load lists every .sav file in
ux0:data/concilia-tower/.
Each save keeps three older backups (.1 .2 .3). Export .TDT writes an
original-format tower file that other SimTower versions can open.
Saves and boot.log stay in ux0:data/concilia-tower/ for all versions.

## Playing Vita Tower
Start with a lobby on the ground floor; everything is reached through it.
Add floors, then offices: office workers pay rent and are your first income.
Condos sell once for a big payment. Fast food and shops feed and entertain
people. Hotel rooms need housekeeping and earn money each night.
Elevators: place a shaft, then drag its top or bottom to extend it. A shaft
serves at most 30 floors; express elevators skip floors and stop only at
lobbies (sky lobbies every 15 floors). Stairs and escalators work for
short trips. Long waits make tenants unhappy and they will move out.
Evaluation: use the Map's Eval tab or Inspect. Red tenants are unhappy -
fix transport, noise or missing services nearby.
Stars: grow the population and add the required facilities (security,
medical center, recycling, metro, ...) to earn stars. More stars unlock
more items. Watch for fires and bomb threats; security helps.
Speed: Play/Pause in Tools or the Speed menu.
